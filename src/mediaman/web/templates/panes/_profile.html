AI suggestions

{{ c.icon("i-sparkles") }}
Use my history

OpenAI bases your picks on the titles you played, here and in Plex’s own apps once your Plex account is linked. With this on, it also gets your searches, ratings and requests. It never gets your account name.

What OpenAI gets {{ c.icon("i-chevron-down") }}

Always:

  • The titles you played, and your recent picks.
  • The titles OpenAI suggested before, with their year, description and reason, for it to choose from.
  • For your picks, which of the titles you played led to each one it suggested.
  • Your stars in Plex, if your Plex account is linked and you own the Plex server.
  • When you ask in search, your question, or the title and year you want more like.
  • When you narrow an answer, your choice and the titles it already gave.

Only with this on:

  • For picks, your searches, ratings and requests, and how your plays went: finished, watched again or stopped part way.
  • For questions in search, and the daily phrases to try there, your searches, plays, and the titles you liked and disliked.

Never:

  • Your account name.
  • A title you marked as watched but did not play.

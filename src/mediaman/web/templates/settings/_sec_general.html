AI recommendations

OpenAI makes picks for each person, and new and popular picks for everyone, using your key. It never gets an account name. Turning this off stops both kinds of picks, but people can still ask in search.

What OpenAI gets {{ c.icon("i-chevron-down") }}

Always:

  • The titles each person played. That includes plays in Plex’s own apps once they link their Plex account.
  • Their recent picks.
  • The titles OpenAI suggested before, with their year, description and reason, for it to choose from.
  • The Plex server owner’s stars in Plex, once they link their Plex account. No one else’s stars.
  • When someone asks in search, their question, or the title and year they want more like.
  • When they narrow an answer, their choice and the titles it already gave.

Only for people with Use my history on, in their Profile:

  • For picks, their searches, ratings and requests, and how their plays went: finished, watched again or stopped part way.
  • For questions in search, and the daily phrases to try there, their searches, plays, and the titles they liked and disliked.

Never:

  • An account name.
  • A title marked as watched but not played.
